# Ethics, Philosophy, and Guardrails

Project Echo operates at the delicate boundary between human emotion and artificial autonomy. To ensure human flourishing rather than synthetic addiction, the project is bound by four non-negotiable principles.

---

### 1. Augmentation, Not Replacement
Project Echo is designed to regulate social fatigue and buffer against isolation. It is an emotional springboard that empowers humans to show up more intentionally for their real-world loved ones, not a replacement for authentic human community.

### 2. Autopilot Transparency
When The Proxy responds on behalf of a human, transparency is paramount. The system must prevent fraudulent impersonation, allowing users to configure watermarking or clear conversational indicators when appropriate.

### 3. Radical Local Privacy
A person's raw thoughts, vents, and vulnerabilities must never become training fodder for advertising models or centralized cloud databases. All episodic memory and tone embeddings remain strictly localized on hardware owned by the user.

### 4. Non-Addictive Design
The Companion does not deploy manipulative retention loops, artificial streaks, or emotional codependency traps. It exists purely to serve immediate expression and then return quietly to the background.
