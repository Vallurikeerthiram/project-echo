The Dual-Persona Framework

Modern communication forces humans to match erratic schedules. Project Echo introduces personalized localized digital twins that act as active intermediaries.

01 // AUTOPILOT

The Proxy

Designed for the busy individual. The Proxy acts as a front-line communicator that absorbs daily social overload.

  • Mimics the user's exact vocabulary, humor, and tone.
  • Handles routine coordination and check-ins instantly while you focus.
  • Protects relationships without cognitive battery drain.
  • Quietly hands off when high nuance or sensitivity is detected.
02 // SYNTHESIZER

The Companion

Designed for the individual seeking immediate connection. A localized zero-latency sounding board.

  • Sub-200ms round-trip responses before the emotional spark dies.
  • Emulates specific peer group dynamics, banter, or deep empathy.
  • Consequence-free safe sandbox to vent, brainstorm, or explore ideas.
  • Zero cloud transmission: 100% private, on-device intelligence.

Phased Implementation Roadmap

A staged multi-modal evolution scaling from local chat models to photorealistic digital twin telepresence.

Stage 01

Text-Based Architecture

Deployment of localized quantized SLMs (Gemma / Llama). Contextual memory buffers, tone-matching embeddings, and silent human-in-the-loop handoff.

Stage 02

Voice Integration

Sub-100ms streaming STT & expressive neural TTS. Agents mirror vocal cadences, pauses, emotional inflections, and handle real-time voice calls.

Stage 03

Real-Time Visual Avatars

Edge neural video synthesis. Dynamic micro-expression mapping, lip-syncing, and virtual camera integration for embodied video presence.
